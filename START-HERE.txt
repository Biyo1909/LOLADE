LOLADE'S BIRTHDAY WEBSITE

1. Extract the ZIP into a folder called Lolade Birthday.
2. Export your two edited videos as MP4, preferably H.264 video with AAC audio.
3. Save the FIRST video as: azidan22.mp4
4. Save the SECOND video as: azidan 23.mp4
   There is ONE SPACE between azidan and 23 in the second filename.
5. Put BOTH videos in the SAME folder as index.html. Do not use a videos subfolder.

Your folder should contain:

Lolade Birthday/
    index.html
    azidan22.mp4
    azidan 23.mp4
    START-HERE.txt

On Windows, turn on File Explorer > View > Show > File name extensions.
Check that the files do not accidentally end in .mp4.mp4.
Renaming a different video format to .mp4 does not convert it: export as MP4.

OPEN AND CHECK
Double-click index.html to preview it in a browser. No installation is needed.
Go through the questions to reach the poem and play both videos.
Until you add the videos, two elegant placeholder cards appear instead.
Internet access loads Cormorant Garamond and Inter; fallback fonts work offline.
On phones, opening a shared HTML attachment may not run JavaScript. For the best
mobile experience, upload index.html and BOTH videos together to a static web
host, preserving their filenames and keeping them in the same directory.
The ZIP currently contains the website and these instructions, not your videos.
If you share the files as a ZIP later, create a new ZIP after adding the videos.

BIRTHDAY GAME
There are six questions, each worth NGN 5,000, for a maximum of NGN 30,000.
Q1 accepts A and D. Q2, Q3 and Q6 accept every answer.
Q4 accepts B. Q5 accepts C.
Each quiz answer locks when selected; it cannot award money multiple times.
Refreshing the page starts a new visit. This is a personal birthday game, not a
secure payment system; the birthday benefactor should verify the amount before
making a transfer.

REWARD CLAIM
The page displays no email address and uses the anonymous-account wording.
The claim form accepts an account name, bank name and 10-digit Nigerian account
number. It opens a prepared email addressed to the recipient you supplied.
Lolade must press Send in her email app; nothing sends automatically.
The recipient address is visible in the email app and in the HTML source.
Mailto requires a configured email app or browser email handler. If nothing
opens, configure one and use the Open my email app again button.
The page cannot confirm that the email was sent and does not transfer money.
The birthday benefactor must make the transfer separately.
Account details are not saved to local storage or sent to a website server;
they remain in page memory until it is closed/refreshed and go into the email.
No bank PIN, password, BVN, OTP or card details are requested.

PRIVACY
Anyone with your hosted link may be able to open or download the videos unless
you enable access protection with your chosen hosting provider. Share carefully.
A truly hidden recipient and automatic claim delivery require a backend or
form service; this single-file version intentionally uses the requested mailto.
